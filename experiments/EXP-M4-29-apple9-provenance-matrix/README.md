# EXP-M4-29: Apple9 producer/consumer provenance closure

This experiment builds a native-Metal, hardware-checked compatibility model
for operand provenance on T8132.  The model is an ordered relation over a
consumer's complete source tuple.  It is intentionally not a table assigning
one route to each opcode or one state to each producing instruction.

All shader sources are generated by this experiment and compiled at runtime
through the public Metal API.  Only the resulting caller-owned binary archives
and their execution results are inspected.  No Apple-authored binary is
disassembled or decompiled.

## Scope

The closure gate covers operand-bearing classes exposed by native Metal in:

- compute shaders;
- vertex shaders, including position and UVS/varying export;
- fragment shaders, including flat, linear, and perspective interpolation,
  ALU consumption, texture/image inputs, and color/depth output.

Mesh/object, ray tracing, matrix operations, and callable-shader ABIs are not
part of this experiment.  A relationship that cannot exist in a stage is
recorded as `stage_inapplicable`, not silently omitted.

## Classification contract

Every type- and stage-applicable matrix cell ends in exactly one of:

- `direct_native`: Metal emits the producer/consumer edge directly and the
  emitted tuple has an exact hardware oracle.
- `native_normalized`: two independent source formulations cause Metal to
  insert the same identified bridge, and the bridged programs execute exactly.
- `native_refused`: the public compiler or pipeline builder explicitly rejects
  a minimal source reproducer; the diagnostic is retained.
- `unresolved`: incomplete evidence.  The final closure gate rejects this.

`optimized_away`, failure to find an instruction, and absence from a corpus are
observations, never aliases for `native_refused`.

## Reproduction overview

Host-only generation and validation:

```sh
python3 generate.py
python3 make_slices.py
python3 verify.py --generated
```

On the current macOS guest, after copying this directory plus the canonical
`tools/shdump` and `tools/agx-isa` directories:

```sh
./build_guest.sh
./run_native_matrix.sh m4-YYYYMMDD-forward forward
./run_native_matrix.sh m4-YYYYMMDD-reverse reverse
```

Back on the host:

```sh
python3 analyze.py captures/m4-YYYYMMDD-forward \
  --repeat captures/m4-YYYYMMDD-reverse --write
python3 verify.py --captured \
  --primary captures/m4-YYYYMMDD-forward \
  --repeat captures/m4-YYYYMMDD-reverse
python3 verify.py --dispatch-package
```

The native census is followed by targeted, coordinated archive ablations.  An
ablation is not promoted unless the unmodified case is exact, a positive
control proves output sensitivity, and every field forming the
producer/consumer tuple is changed coherently.

The default `seed` generation is intentionally not a closure claim.  For a
same-type multi-source consumer it emits only homogeneous tuples `(P, P, ...)`,
plus two semantically identical source spellings, every one-role retained
form, and representative distant/control-flow/pressure arms.  A genuinely
mixed-type form receives one canonical per-type tuple.  `--mode full` expands
lifetime masks only; heterogeneous producer tuples are a later phase once the
producer inventory has converged.  This prevents early source-family guesses
from multiplying into a corpus that is too large to inspect.

## Files

- `model.py`: canonical enums, provisional classes, cases, and schemas.
- `generate.py`: deterministic MSL/case/oracle generator.
- `make_slices.py`: reproducible focused and greedy homogeneous-coverage
  slices tied to the complete corpus hash, including bounded class-equivalence
  and distinguishing-consumer panels.
- `native_runner.m`: generic compute/render archive executor.  It accepts
  direct 1D/3D and indirect threadgroup dispatch dimensions; omitted fields
  preserve the original direct 64×32 behavior.  Optional `--snapshot` sends
  precommit and postcomplete signals for caller-owned package capture.
- `run_native_matrix.sh`: fresh-process forward/reverse capture runner.
- `prepare_ablations.py`, `run_ablations.py`: stage-relative mutation
  materializer and repeated archive-input executor.
- `analyze.py`: archive/disassembly census and conservative classification.
- `summarize_signatures.py`: exact producer/stage ALU/store fingerprints and
  constant/device merge-candidate comparisons.
- `summarize_candidate_pairs.py`: order-stable, all-symbol comparisons for the
  bounded constant/device distinguishing-consumer and fanout panel.
- `summarize_vector_widths.py`: scalar-result publication comparisons across
  scalar, uint2, and uint4 source loads.
- `summarize_gpr_candidates.py`: exact ordinary-ALU, predicate-result, and
  raw-system-index comparison report; absent expected families are recorded
  without treating absence as normalization or refusal.
- `build_gpr_route_ablations.py` and `summarize_gpr_ablations.py`: repeated
  terminal-ISELECT route census for ALU and materialized-predicate results,
  including independent selected-register controls and intermittent
  SIMD-group corruption accounting.
- `summarize_return_candidates.py`: buffer/texture/threadgroup/atomic/subgroup
  return comparison report with the terminal dependent-load record isolated
  from its source producer.
- `build_return_route_ablations.py`,
  `build_return_sensitivity_ablations.py`, and
  `summarize_return_ablations.py`: exhaustive compute-ISELECT route census,
  independent semantic-sensitivity controls, and a checked route-signature
  report for the return-producer panel.
- `summarize_fragment_uniforms.py`: all-symbol comparison of ordinary indexed
  fragment loads and invocation-invariant fixed-index loads, including main
  versus constant-program placement and uniform-preload use.
- `summarize_fragment_booleans.py`: pairwise all-symbol comparison of fragment
  ALU results, materialized predicates, and the front-facing system value.
- `summarize_compute_immediates.py`: exact immediate-versus-runtime-ALU census
  separating compile-time folding from retained literal materialization.
- `make_float_immediate_texture_supplement.py`: independent alternate-value
  float-coordinate workload used to locate the native literal payload.
- `build_immediate_materialization_ablations.py`: exact controls, integer
  load-bearing-prefix mutations, float non-payload-prefix control, and native
  alternate-payload recode.
- `summarize_immediate_materialization.py` and
  `promote_immediate_classifications.py`: checked 43-cell normalization report
  and deterministic classification promotion.
- `make_compute_system_values_supplement.py`,
  `build_compute_system_value_ablations.py`, and
  `summarize_compute_system_values.py`: compact public-builtin comparison,
  cross-native system-register selector recodes, and checked provenance-group
  decision.
- `make_compute_dispatch_geometry_supplement.py` and
  `summarize_compute_dispatch_geometry.py`: three-geometry runtime packaging
  census for public compute system values.
- `make_dispatch_package_ablations.py`, `dispatch_package_iotrace.patch`, and
  `summarize_compute_dispatch_package.py`: exact caller-owned package capture,
  one-u32 mutation, and full-buffer validation of the CDM/global/local geometry
  contract used by `threadgroups_per_grid.x`.
- `make_dispatch_package_3d_supplement.py`,
  `make_dispatch_package_3d_ablations.py`, and
  `summarize_compute_dispatch_package_3d.py`: one asymmetric 3D shader and six
  bounded component-wise package runs establishing the tuple extension without
  a heterogeneous-source expansion.
- `make_dispatch_package_indirect_supplement.py`,
  `make_dispatch_package_indirect_ablations.py`, and
  `summarize_compute_dispatch_package_indirect.py`: one asymmetric indirect
  record and four bounded runs separating the raw record, metadata scale, and
  CDM local divisor while reusing the qualified 3D shader archive.
- `make_raw_system_index_supplement.py`,
  `build_raw_system_index_ablations.py`, and
  `summarize_raw_system_index.py`: hash-closed unmasked system-index address
  cases, selected-register sensitivity control, and checked result report.
- `make_back_facing_supplement.py` and
  `summarize_front_facing_winding.py`: front/back winding crossed with two
  independent front-facing IADD source formulations and full-target oracles.
- `make_range_controlled_boolean_supplement.py`,
  `build_range_controlled_ablations.py`, and
  `summarize_range_controlled_boolean.py`: equivalent runtime 0/1 ALU/predicate
  sources, exhaustive ISELECT route census, and provisional bit-extract class
  decision.
- `verify.py`: source, repeatability, oracle, and closure gates.
- `producer_classes.json`, `consumer_classes.json`, `matrix.json`: generated
  machine-readable model.
- `CLASS_DECISIONS.json`: conservative producer merges, retained splits, and
  the evidence required to revisit each decision.
- `PROVENANCE_GROUPS.json`: architectural producer groups after source-level
  construction variants are collapsed by native and hardware evidence.
- `IMMEDIATE_MATERIALIZATION_RESULTS.json`: exact folding/materialization
  bridges, retained family signatures, and repeated ablation outcomes.
- `COMPUTE_SYSTEM_VALUE_RESULTS.json`: exact 40-cell builtin signatures,
  selector metadata, retained construction splits, and 24 ablation executions.
- `COMPUTE_DISPATCH_GEOMETRY_RESULTS.json`: cross-geometry binary stability,
  exact dynamic oracles, and dispatch-state construction witnesses.
- `COMPUTE_DISPATCH_PACKAGE_RESULTS.json`: three package snapshots and five
  single-field ablations resolving global-count publication, local-size input,
  invocation count, and native ceiling division.
- `COMPUTE_DISPATCH_PACKAGE_3D_RESULTS.json`: asymmetric 3D package and shader
  evidence for the X/Y/Z metadata, CDM, selector, and ceiling-division rules.
- `COMPUTE_DISPATCH_PACKAGE_INDIRECT_RESULTS.json`: exact direct/indirect
  normalization contract and the generation-specific indirect CDM layout.
- `PROVENANCE_MODEL.md`: derived architectural model.
- `RESULTS.md`: observed evidence, conflicts, and final closure status.
- `SUPERSESSION_LEDGER.json`: machine-readable treatment of older experiments
  and rejected/superseded EXP-M4-29 runs.
